## Summary by Domain

**Emerging Patterns Across Industries (2020-2025):**

- **AI/ML Integration**: Fault prediction, anomaly detection, and digital twins now standard
- **Sensor Technology**: 5G, IoT edge computing, fiber optics enabling real-time data collection
- **Cloud & Analytics**: Federated learning, cloud data lakes, automated reporting
- **Autonomous Systems**: Drones, robots for inspection; reducing human risk
- **Digital Twins**: Virtual replicas driving maintenance optimization across all sectors
- **Predictive RUL**: Remaining useful life estimation replacing time-based schedules
- **Sustainability**: Emissions tracking, energy optimization, carbon accounting integrated
