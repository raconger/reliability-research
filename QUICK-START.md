# Quick Start: Transfer Encyclopedia to Kindle

## 🚀 Fastest Method (2 minutes)

### Step 1: Find Your Kindle Email
Go to: https://www.amazon.com/hz/mycd/digital-console/contentlist/booksAll/dateDsc/
- Click **Preferences** → **Personal Document Settings**
- Find your Kindle email (looks like: `yourname@kindle.com`)

### Step 2: Add Your Email to Approved List
On the same page:
- Scroll to "Approved Personal Document E-mail List"
- Add the email address you'll send from

### Step 3: Email the Book
- **File:** `ebook-output/Reliability-Asset-Management-Encyclopedia.epub`
- **To:** Your Kindle email address
- **Send!**

### Step 4: Wait 5-10 Minutes
The book will automatically appear on your Kindle!

---

## 📱 Alternative: USB Cable

1. Connect Kindle to computer
2. Copy `ebook-output/Reliability-Asset-Management-Encyclopedia.epub` to **Documents** folder
3. Eject Kindle
4. Done!
